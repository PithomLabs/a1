# Calculate overtime pay for a monthly-rated employee

If you are a monthly-rated employee covered under Part 4 of the Employment Act, use this calculator to find out your pay for working overtime.

You can claim overtime if you are:

*   A non-workman earning a monthly [basic salary](/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered#who-is-covered-under-part-iv) of $2,600 or less.
*   A workman earning a monthly [basic salary](/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered#who-is-covered-under-part-iv) of $4,500 or less.

Generally, a workman is someone whose work involves mainly manual labour. [Find out if you fall under this category](https://safe.menlosecurity.com/https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered#who-is-a-workman).

[Overtime work](/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days#overtime-pay) is all work in excess of your normal hours of work (excluding breaks).
