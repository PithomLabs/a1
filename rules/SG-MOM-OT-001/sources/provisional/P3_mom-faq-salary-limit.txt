# Is there a salary limit for overtime pay entitlement?

[Overtime pay](/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days#overtime-pay) is compulsory if an employee is:

*   A non-workman earning a monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less.
*   A [workman](/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered#who-is-a-workman) earning a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less.
